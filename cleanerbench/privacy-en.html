Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026.

Developer and support

Sergey Petruk operates Photo Bench and is responsible for processing data. For help with the app or questions about your data, email support@pishiverno.ru with “Photo Bench” in the subject.

Photos, videos and contacts

Photo Bench requests access to your media library or contacts when you start the corresponding scan. It analyzes your library and contacts on your device. The app does not send photos, videos or the contents of your contacts to the developer or to Firebase.

Scan results, indexes, deletion history and backups for contact merges are stored on your device in the app. Changes you confirm in the system Photos or Contacts apps may sync through iCloud or other connected accounts according to your device settings. You can change photo and contacts permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting Photo Bench removes its local data. You can manage changes already made to photos or contacts in those system apps.

Apps tab

Photo Bench checks whether a limited list of supported apps with shortcuts to cleanup settings is installed. The result stays on your device. It is not sent to analytics or used for advertising. You clear the cache in the third-party app.

Analytics and crash reports

Sharing is off by default. If you enable it in Privacy, Google Analytics for Firebase receives usage events such as launching the app, completing onboarding, switching between the Photos, Videos and Contacts tabs, and opening Pro. The SDK may collect in-app purchase information, app and system versions, device model, approximate region and an installation identifier. Firebase Crashlytics receives technical crash reports, including stack traces and device details, so we can find and fix bugs.

Google processes this data to provide Analytics and Crashlytics under Firebase’s privacy terms. Photo Bench does not use this data for personalized advertising or collect the iOS advertising identifier.

Google Analytics retains detailed event data according to its property retention settings. Standard aggregated reports may be retained longer. Crashlytics retains stack traces and associated identifiers for up to 90 days before initiating deletion. If you contact support, we use your email address and message to respond and retain them as long as needed to handle your request and comply with applicable requirements.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and one-time purchases through the App Store under Apple’s privacy policy. Photo Bench uses StoreKit purchase status to grant Pro access and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Privacy choices and requests

Tap the hand icon on the main screen to open Privacy. Select “Don’t share” under Analytics and crash reports to stop further sharing. To request access to your data or its deletion, contact support. If we cannot match data to your request, we will let you know.
