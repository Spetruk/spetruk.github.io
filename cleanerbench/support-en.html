What can I do for free?

Scan and review your photos and videos for free. You can delete up to 10 files in total across photos and videos. Reviewing and merging duplicate contacts is free. Pro removes the limit on photo and video deletion and adds shortcuts to cleanup settings in supported apps.

How do I allow access to photos or contacts?

Open Settings → Apps → ByteHole and adjust access to Photos or Contacts. With limited access, the app can scan only the items you allow. Return to the relevant tab to update the results.

Why is a photo blurry or unavailable?

ByteHole uses images already available on your iPhone. If an original is stored only in iCloud, the app may show a smaller local preview. Open the original in Apple's Photos app to download it, then return to ByteHole.

I deleted files, but available storage has barely changed

The app shows the size of selected or deleted files. The space they occupy on the iPhone can differ when iCloud storage optimization is enabled. Deleted files may remain in Recently Deleted in Photos. Deletions can also sync to iCloud and your other devices. Review Recently Deleted in Photos before permanently removing anything.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Pro and tap Restore purchases while using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Pro is available as a weekly subscription or a one-time purchase. Send support the app version and the message shown if restoration fails.

How do I manage my subscription?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions and select ByteHole. Apple manages billing and subscription cancellation. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I control analytics and crash reports?

Sharing is off by default. You can change Analytics and crash reports in Settings → Apps → ByteHole.
